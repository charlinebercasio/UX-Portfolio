EXPLORATION · GAMIFICATION

Play, on purpose: four gamified experiences

Three concepts that add a game layer to existing projects, plus one standalone styling game. Every phone is a working prototype, so tap through them. Each concept starts from the behaviour it should encourage, not from points and badges.

Option 1–3 · Game layers for existing products

Solenne · loyalty
Solenne Style Quest

Weekly quests, a streak and a tier-up spin wheel turn the loyalty program into something you play.

Try

Check in, review the trousers, then style a look under $200. You'll cross into Atelier and get to spin.

Psychology

Goal-gradient effect: the closer the next tier looks, the harder people push. The progress bar does the motivating.

Why not just points

Quests reward behaviours the business wants, like reviews and building outfits, not only spending.

Playpals · family
Playpals Play Streaks

Log a play session, collect a sticker, and finish the Playpack to open a mystery toy.

Try

Pick an activity for today, watch the sticker land, then tap the gift box three times.

Psychology

Variable rewards and collection: stickers build a visible record of time spent together, and the mystery toy is the surprise.

Why not just points

It rewards play with your kid, not app opens. The streak counts days of play, so the app wins only when the family does.

Sleepmaxx · wellbeing
Sleepmaxx Sleep Garden

A plant grows every night you stick to your wind-down. Miss a night and it droops, but it never dies.

Try

Skip a night to see the droop, use a rain day, then finish the week and plant a new seed.

Psychology

Loss aversion, used gently. A wilting plant nudges you without the shame of a broken streak, and the rain day builds in grace.

Why not just points

Sleep is health. Punishing streaks make anxious sleepers worse, so this one forgives.

OPTION 4 · STANDALONE GAME
DRESS CODE

Dress to impress, but with real dress codes, inside a Y2K desktop. Open an invite, style an alt Y2K doll from a closet of baby tees, lace corsets, plaid minis and buckle platforms before the timer runs out, then walk the runway. Three judges score dress code, vibe and colour in a buddy chat, and coins unlock new pieces.

The loop

Invite → read the code → style in 90 seconds → runway → judges → coins → unlock → next invite.

Scoring you can learn

Each piece has a formality level and vibes. The judges explain what cost you stars, so every round teaches you something.

Art direction

I art-directed the doll and closet with Canva AI: one base doll plus each piece as its own illustration, then fitted every piece to her body in the game.

Where it could go

A brand could plug in its real catalogue, so a winning look becomes "shop this outfit".

Try: open Garden wedding, then put on the satin slip and knee boots. Then try studded shorts and no shoes and read what the judges say.
